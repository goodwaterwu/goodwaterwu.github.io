8-Week Firmware Engineer Interview Training Plan

This 8-week training plan is designed to help you prepare for firmware engineer interviews at top tech companies. It includes a mix of LeetCode and HackerRank problems, focusing on relevant topics and excluding Hard difficulty questions. Company abbreviations are: G (Google), A (Amazon), M (Meta), HR (HackerRank Generic Firmware), HR-Bit (HackerRank Bit Manipulation).
